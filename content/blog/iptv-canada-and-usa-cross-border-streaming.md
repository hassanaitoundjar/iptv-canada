## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need two IPTV subscriptions — one for Canada and one for the US?**
No. A single IPTV Canada subscription includes both Canadian and American channels. Your credentials work in both countries.

**Will my IPTV work at my hotel in the US?**
Yes. As long as the hotel has Wi-Fi, you can connect your Fire TV Stick to the hotel TV and stream your channels. Some hotels require you to authenticate through their Wi-Fi portal first — connect your phone to the hotel Wi-Fi, complete the sign-in, then connect your Fire TV Stick.

**Do I need a VPN to use IPTV in the US?**
Not required. Your IPTV subscription works without a VPN in both Canada and the United States. However, a VPN is recommended if you're on public Wi-Fi (airports, hotels, cafes) for privacy and security.

**Can my spouse watch in Canada while I watch in the US?**
Depending on your plan, you may have 1–2 simultaneous connections. Check your subscription tier — some plans support multi-device streaming so one person can watch in Toronto while another watches in Fort Lauderdale.

**What about Mexico or the Caribbean?**
IPTV works anywhere with an internet connection. If you winter in Mexico, Costa Rica, or the Caribbean, your channels work the same as they do in the US.

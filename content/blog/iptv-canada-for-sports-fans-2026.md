## Optimizing Your Setup for Live Sports

Live sports demand a stable, low-latency connection. Here is how to avoid buffering during crucial moments:

### Internet Speed Requirements

| Quality | Minimum Speed | Recommended Speed |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Definition | 5 Mbps | 10 Mbps |
| HD (1080p) | 10 Mbps | 25 Mbps |
| 4K Ultra HD | 25 Mbps | 50+ Mbps |

### Reduce Buffering During Games

1. **Use a wired Ethernet connection** whenever possible. Wi-Fi is fine for movies, but live sports need consistent bandwidth
2. **Close other streaming apps** on your network during game time
3. **Restart your device before a big game** to clear the cache
